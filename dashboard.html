Sydney Suburbs — Property Sales Screener

Median price and 1yr / 5yr growth by suburb, dwelling type and bedroom count, for every suburb within 60 km of Sydney CBD.

Suburb names, regions and distances are real (Australia Post / ABS locality data, straight-line distance from Sydney CBD). Median/min/max price, growth, days on market, recent sales and the development score are synthetic placeholders — median/min/max/growth are modeled per dwelling type and bedroom count as a smooth curve of distance from the CBD (with a hand-curated correction for ~80 known waterfront suburbs) plus random noise; days on market is a distance/growth-based estimate with no listings data behind it at all; "recent sales" are illustrative, not real listings; the development score is a documented weighted heuristic (see a suburb's detail panel) with no access to real zoning, lot sizes, or council data. None of this is sourced from actual sales or planning records — run nsw_sales_pipeline.py to replace the house/unit median/min/max with live NSW Valuer General figures (that dataset has no bedroom count or listing date at all, so the bedroom breakdown and days-on-market stay modeled placeholders), and verify any development score against council/professional advice before acting on it.
