Sydney Suburbs — Property Sales Screener

Median price and 1yr / 5yr growth by suburb, dwelling type and bedroom count, for every suburb within 60 km of Sydney CBD.

Suburb names, regions and distances are real (Australia Post / ABS locality data, straight-line distance from Sydney CBD). Median price, growth, recent sales and the development score are synthetic placeholders — median/growth are modeled per dwelling type and bedroom count as a smooth curve of distance from the CBD plus random noise; "recent sales" are illustrative, not real listings; the development score is a documented weighted heuristic (see a suburb's detail panel) with no access to real zoning, lot sizes, or council data. None of this is sourced from actual sales or planning records — run nsw_sales_pipeline.py to replace the house/unit medians with live NSW Valuer General figures (that dataset has no bedroom count at all, so the bedroom breakdown stays a modeled placeholder), and verify any development score against council/professional advice before acting on it.
