Suburb names, regions and distances are real (Australia Post / ABS locality data, straight-line distance from Sydney CBD). Median price, growth, recent sales and the development score are synthetic placeholders — median/growth are modeled as a smooth curve of distance from the CBD plus random noise; "recent sales" are illustrative, not real listings; the development score is a documented weighted heuristic (see a suburb's detail panel) with no access to real zoning, lot sizes, or council data. None of this is sourced from actual sales or planning records — run nsw_sales_pipeline.py to replace it with live NSW Valuer General figures, and verify any development score against council/professional advice before acting on it.
